Executive Context & Dual Assignment Structure

This portal integrates two comprehensive data systems:

  • Human BI Dashboard: An executive-level personal analytics cockpit modeled after enterprise Power BI reporting systems, quantifying faculty leadership, military reserve readiness, student reach, and holistic well-being.

Human BI Dashboard: Architecture & KPI Formulas

A. 3x3 Asymmetric Executive Layout

The dashboard follows Microsoft Power BI's standard visual hierarchy:

  • Row 1 (Current State): Identity profile [A], high-level operational KPIs [B], and longitudinal temporal trends [C].
  • Row 2 (Diagnostic Analysis): Underlying insights [D], anomaly diagnosis [E], and strategic inquiry [F].
  • Row 3 (Future State & Governance): 6-month statistical forecast [G], data governance & ethics [H], and leadership identity [I].

B. Index Normalization & Anomaly Correlation

The Professional Impact Index and Personal Well-being Index are calculated on a standardized 0–100 scale:

Impact Index = (0.40 × Student Reach) + (0.35 × Projects Active) + (0.25 × Learning Hours)
Well-being Index = (0.50 × Physical Readiness Score) + (0.30 × Recovery Hours) + (0.20 × Work-Life Equilibrium)

April Anomaly Discovery: In April, Professional Impact peaked at 80 while Personal Well-being dipped to 68. Statistical cross-referencing revealed the confluence of academic finals grading and rigorous reserve military training cycles.

QuakeSpots GIS: Spatial Statistics & Mathematical Models

A. Getis-Ord Gi* Spatial Statistics (Hot Spot Analysis)

Identifies statistically significant spatial clusters of high values (hot spots) and low values (cold spots) using the local $G_i^*$ statistic:

G_i^*(d) = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^n w_{ij}(d) x_j - \bar{X} \sum_{j=1}^n w_{ij}(d)}{S \sqrt{\frac{n \sum_{j=1}^n w_{ij}^2(d) - (\sum_{j=1}^n w_{ij}(d))^2}{n - 1}}}

Points with z-scores $> +2.58$ represent Hot Spots at 99% Confidence (crimson markers), while $z < -2.58$ represent Cold Spots at 99% Confidence (deep blue markers).

B. Anselin Local Moran's I (LISA)

Detects spatial autocorrelation and local spatial outliers by classifying point pairs into four distinct quadrants:

  • High-High (HH): High magnitude earthquake clustered in high-magnitude vicinity (True Hot Spot).
  • Low-Low (LL): Minor tremors surrounded by minor tremors (Cold Spot).
  • High-Low (HL): High magnitude event surrounded by low values (Spatial Outlier).
  • Low-High (LH): Low magnitude event surrounded by high values (Spatial Outlier).

C. Emerging Hot Spot Analysis (EHSA)

Tracks temporal persistence and intensification of earthquake activity over space-time bins, categorizing clusters into Intensifying, Persistent, Sporadic, and Cold Spots.

D. Dual Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)

Generates continuous probability density surfaces across two separate weightings:

  • Spatial Frequency (Event Density): Pure spatial occurrence density (blue → lime → red).
  • Magnitude Energy Release: Energy-weighted density via seismic moment release (lightblue → orange → darkred).
